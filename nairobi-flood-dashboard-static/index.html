The story in three lines

  1. Losses grow fast with rarer storms. A 250-year flood costs roughly 5.7× a 10-year one.
  2. Poorer housing takes the biggest hit. Informal and semi-permanent homes carry most of the loss, so accumulation clusters in dense settlements.
  3. The model misses drainage-driven flooding. It only sees terrain and rivers, so half the known hotspots go undetected — real losses are probably higher.

Methodology & limitations

This preview uses a terrain-and-river hazard proxy, not measured flood depths. The building portfolio and monetary loss values are synthetic examples. Real drainage reports and the production model are not connected; treat all estimates as illustrative, not as underwriting or emergency-response advice.
