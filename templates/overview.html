Overview

Urban flood risk snapshot

This dashboard translates a flood catastrophe model into a fast, plain-English view for underwriters, portfolio managers, and county stakeholders. It makes the core risk story legible in under two minutes, with results framed as a synthetic portfolio analysis supported by a hazard proxy and AI-assisted drainage signals.

Data transparency

  • Portfolio: SYNTHETIC — 600 invented buildings, total insured value KES 6,363,470,000.
  • Hazard layer: PROXY — susceptibility score 0 to 1 from elevation and OpenStreetMap rivers. Not a calibrated flood model.
  • Vulnerability: adapted from public JRC/Huizinga curves because no Kenya-specific depth-damage curves are available.
  • Reporting: all numbers in this sample are illustrative and clearly labelled as SAMPLE DATA when MOCK_MODE is enabled.
